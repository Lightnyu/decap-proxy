Cookie Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy explains how White Truffle Project Ltd uses cookies and similar technologies on wtpmed.com.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files or similar identifiers stored on, or accessed from, your device when you visit a website. They can be used to make a website function, remember choices, protect security and measure how a site is used.

Essential technologies

Some technologies may be necessary for the operation, security and administration of the website. These cannot always be switched off through our website settings because the site may not work correctly without them.

Analytics and similar technologies

Where we use analytics, heatmaps, session-replay or similar technologies that are not strictly necessary, we will configure them in line with the consent choices presented to visitors. Our analytics setup may change as the website develops, and this policy will be updated when new tools are introduced.

Third-party services

Links to third-party services, including payment platforms, may lead to websites that set their own cookies. Those services are responsible for explaining their own cookie practices.

Managing your choices

Where a cookie-control panel is available, you can use it to accept, reject or revisit non-essential cookie choices. You can also control cookies through your browser settings, although blocking some cookies may affect website functionality.

Updates

We will update this policy when our cookie or analytics technologies materially change.
