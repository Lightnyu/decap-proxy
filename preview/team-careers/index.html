Compliance

Este

Este leads the development of WTP’s compliance processes, policies and supporting records. Her role helps translate operational requirements into clear procedures, organised documentation and practical oversight. Working with scientific, nursery and business colleagues, she supports consistent standards as operations develop, and contributes to the planning and delivery of the Roots of Education programme.

Nursery and Agriculture Manager

François

François leads nursery and farm planning, helping establish the growing operations that connect tree production with future orchard development. His role covers practical workflows, plant care, traceability and coordination of operational teams.
