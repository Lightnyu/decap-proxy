Effective date: 30 June 2026

1. The programme

These terms apply to the White Truffle Project Member Referral Programme. By submitting a referral, you accept these terms.

2. Eligibility

The programme is available to current White Truffle Project members aged 18 or over. Employees, contractors, agents and their immediate families may be excluded at WTP’s discretion.

3. Qualifying referral

A qualifying referral is a genuinely new customer, not already known to WTP, who completes the applicable qualifying purchase, has a fully executed agreement and whose purchase is not cancelled, refunded, disputed or reversed.

4. Tree award and timing

One verified qualifying referral earns one fully paid managed truffle tree. It is allocated after verification. The award is not cash and cannot be exchanged or transferred unless WTP agrees in writing.

5. Verification and exclusions

WTP’s records determine attribution. No award is made for duplicate, self, fraudulent, incomplete, cancelled, refunded or non-qualifying referrals.

6. No authority or advice

Referrers are not WTP employees, agents, representatives or financial advisers. They have no authority to bind WTP and must not give financial, legal or tax advice or make promises about harvests, income, value or returns.

7. Privacy and consent

Only provide a person’s details where they have agreed to WTP contacting them. Marketing consent, where required, must be obtained directly from the referred person.

8. Tax

Referrers are responsible for their own tax position and reporting obligations.

9. Changes and termination

WTP may amend, suspend or end the programme where reasonably necessary, including for fraud prevention, legal requirements or operational reasons.

10. Complaints and governing law

Send concerns to info@wtpmed.com. These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.
