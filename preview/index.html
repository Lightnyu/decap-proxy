HOW HOBBY FARMER WORKS

Three steps. One managed journey.

01

You build ownership

Each £200 monthly payment purchases one individually recorded truffle-inoculated tree during your current cycle.

02

WTP manages the orchard

We provide the contracted orchard infrastructure, planting, care and harvesting services so you do not have to operate an orchard yourself.

03

You review before continuing

The first cycle is 36 months. At each renewal point, you decide whether to continue the longer 12-year route under the programme terms.

WHAT YOU ACTUALLY OWN

Your trees.
Individually recorded.

You own identifiable trees — not shares in White Truffle Project, units in a pooled fund, a pension or a savings account.

WTP manages the orchard under contract. Your trees remain biological assets, and their future production and market value can vary.

Harvest participation and WTP’s management share are governed by the customer agreement.
