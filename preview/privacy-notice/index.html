Privacy Notice

Last updated: 8 October 2026

White Truffle Project Ltd (“WTP”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is the controller of personal information collected through this website and through enquiries about our programmes and services.

Who we are

White Truffle Project Ltd, company number 16522816, registered in England and Wales. Registered office: 14 Museum Place, Cardiff, Wales, CF10 3BH. You can contact us at info@wtpmed.com.

Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may collect contact details, enquiry information, information you provide when requesting documents or joining a programme, transaction and payment-reference information supplied by payment providers, and technical information generated when you use the website.

Why we use personal information

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, provide requested information, administer customer relationships and agreements, process and reconcile payments, maintain business records, protect the website and our systems, comply with legal obligations, and improve our services where lawful to do so.

Lawful bases

Our lawful bases may include taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, performing a contract, complying with legal obligations, our legitimate interests in operating and protecting our business, and consent where consent is required.

Who we may share information with

We may share information with service providers that support our website, communications, payment processing, customer administration, professional advisers, insurers, banks, auditors, and public authorities where required by law. We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

Payments and external services

Some payment or application links may take you to a third-party service such as Stripe. Those providers process information under their own privacy terms as well as any processing they carry out on our behalf.

International transfers

Some service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where this occurs, we take appropriate steps to use lawful transfer mechanisms and safeguards where required.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including contractual, accounting, tax, regulatory and legal requirements. Different categories of information may have different retention periods.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal information, restrict or object to processing, receive certain information in a portable format, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise a privacy right, contact info@wtpmed.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we use your information, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our services, legal requirements or data-processing activities change. The latest version will be published on this page.
