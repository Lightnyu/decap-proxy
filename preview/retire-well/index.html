A DIFFERENT WAY TO OWN AGRICULTURAL ASSETS

Direct ownership. Specialist management.

Hobby Farmer is a long-term tree-ownership programme created by White Truffle Project. Instead of buying land or operating an orchard yourself, you acquire individually recorded truffle trees while WTP provides the contracted orchard infrastructure, planting, management and harvesting services.

Your participation also supports the expansion of WTP’s truffle-production capacity and the company’s wider scientific mission.

You own trees - not shares in WTP, units in a pooled fund or a guaranteed financial return.

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Before you begin

1. Do I own shares in White Truffle Project?

No. You own individually recorded trees, not shares in White Truffle Project. Under the current programme model, your contractual harvest entitlement begins after Year 12 and provides a 55% share of attributable harvest, subject to the customer agreement.

2. Do I need to own land?

No. You do not necessarily need to own land to participate. The project structure is designed to allow participants to take part without purchasing or owning agricultural land themselves.

3. Are harvests or returns guaranteed?
No. Harvests, production levels, timing, quality, market value, and financial returns are not guaranteed. Truffle production is biological and agricultural in nature and is subject to weather, growing conditions, management, market conditions, and other risks.
4. What happens after the initial 36 months?
At the end of each 36-month cycle, you decide whether to enter the next cycle. If you do not renew, there is no automatic obligation to continue future monthly purchases. Rights in trees already acquired continue subject to the customer agreement.
5. When might trees begin producing?
Trees may biologically begin producing before Year 12, but under the current Hobby Farmer model contractual harvest entitlement begins after 12 years from programme commencement. Biological production, timing and harvest outcomes are not guaranteed.
6. What happens if I cannot complete a 36-month cycle?

If you cannot complete your current 36-month cycle, the programme includes conditional options: an agreed payment pause of up to six months; WTP repurchase of eligible incomplete-cycle trees at £200 per tree, subject to the applicable conditions; or conversion to Whole Ownership by paying an additional £499 per retained tree under the separate Whole Ownership Agreement. These options have important conditions and financial consequences. Please review the sample Customer Agreement and Risk Guide for details.
